# If something goes wrong: Research a contact or company

What a refusal means, and what to do next. Tell the person what happened in plain words.

- **Your AI client says the service is not set up or not allowed.** Your CRM limits which outside services the AI may reach. Ask the team that runs your CRM.
- **A fact did not change the record.** The evidence was not strong enough, or a person typed the value. Open the contact's **Agent** tab, select **Facts**, and accept or dismiss the fact.
- **A photo did not come.** No source is tied to the contact yet: no GitHub account, no Gravatar for the email address and no image on the company's website. Add what you know to the record first.
- **The contact did not move to the new company.** The new employer is not a company in the CRM, the evidence was weak, or a person linked the current company. Move the contact yourself in the app.
