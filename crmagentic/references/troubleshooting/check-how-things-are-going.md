# If something goes wrong: Check how things are going

What a refusal means, and what to do next. Tell the person what happened in plain words.

- **Your AI client cannot read the pipeline or the reports.** Its key cannot read deals. Ask your admin for a key whose roles include deals.
- **Your AI client says it cannot read a report.** No tool reads that screen yet. Open the screen it names.
- **A campaign has no send counts.** The campaign is a draft or scheduled, and it has not sent yet.
