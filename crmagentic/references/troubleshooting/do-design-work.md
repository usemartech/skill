# If something goes wrong: Do design work handed to you

What a refusal means, and what to do next. Tell the person what happened in plain words.

- **Your AI client says someone else is already on the item.** Another person or agent started it. Ask them, or cancel the item and add a new one.
- **Your AI client cannot mark the item done.** The item names a block that is no longer on the page or in the email. Cancel the item, or add a new one for the whole design.
- **Your AI client lists no work.** The items are assigned to someone else, or they are done or cancelled. Check **Assign to** on the item in the designer.
- **The agent is not in the Assign to list.** The agent has no role, or it is switched off, or the AI agent is off in your workspace (then **Assign to** lists people only). Ask the team that runs your CRM.
