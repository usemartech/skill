# Check how things are going

From the user guide: Check how things are going with your AI client.

You ask your AI client how your sales and your marketing are going. It reads the same numbers as the dashboard and the reports, and it sends you to the screen for a chart or a report that no tool reads.

## Before you start

- Connect your AI client.
- A **read only** key is enough.
- The sales numbers need a key that can read deals.

## AI does through MCP

1. Ask how your sales are going. Tools: `crm_dashboard_summary`, `crm_dashboard_pipeline`, `crm_dashboard_forecast`.
2. Ask what is overdue and what happened lately. Tools: `crm_dashboard_recent`, `crm_dashboard_marketing`.
3. Ask how your reports look. Tools: `crm_report_overview`, `crm_report_accounts`, `crm_form_report`.
4. Ask how your email campaigns did. Tools: `crm_email_campaign_list`, `crm_email_campaign_get`.
5. Ask about one form, list, funnel or source. Tools: `crm_form_list`, `crm_form_get`, `crm_list_get`, `crm_funnel_get`, `crm_source_get`, `crm_deal_list`, `crm_task_list`.

## The person does in the app

- For a chart, or a report that no tool reads, your AI client sends you to its screen. In the sidebar, select **Core CRM → Dashboard** (/dashboard) for the bars of each stage and the chart of closed deals, and **Forms** then **Report** for the chart of submissions per day. Select **Sources & traffic**, **Shopping carts** or **Social listening** for your traffic, the sales of your store and what people say about you.

If a tool refuses, read references/troubleshooting/check-how-things-are-going.md.
