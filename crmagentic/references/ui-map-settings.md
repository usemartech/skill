# Where the settings are in the app

Each line is the path a person follows, the address, and the guide page about it. Hand a step back with the path exactly as written here. Never guess a path that is not in this map. The sidebar and the other screens are in references/ui-map.md.

## Settings
- **Settings → Workflow snippets** (/settings/workflows/snippets): Set up fields, tags and snippets with your AI client
- **Settings → My profile** (/settings/profile): Your profile. Full steps: https://crmagentic-manual.web.app/marketer/getting-started/your-profile/
- **Settings → Workspace** (/settings/workspace)
- **Settings → Users** (/settings/users)
- **Settings → Roles** (/settings/roles)
- **Settings → Currencies** (/settings/currencies)
- **Settings → Pipelines** (/settings/pipelines): Pipelines and stages with your AI client
- **Settings → Personas** (/settings/personas): Personas. Full steps: https://crmagentic-manual.web.app/marketer/segments/personas/
- **Settings → Tags** (/settings/tags): Tags. Full steps: https://crmagentic-manual.web.app/marketer/segments/tags/
- **Settings → Products and services** (/settings/products): Products and services. Full steps: https://crmagentic-manual.web.app/marketer/commerce/products-and-services/
- **Settings → Sign-in** (/settings/sign-in)
- **Settings → API keys** (/settings/api): Connect another system. Full steps: https://crmagentic-manual.web.app/marketer/getting-started/connect-another-system/
- **Settings → Audit log** (/settings/audit)
- **Settings → Background jobs** (/settings/jobs): Webhooks and background jobs with your AI client
- **Settings → Lead scoring** (/settings/scoring): Lead scoring. Full steps: https://crmagentic-manual.web.app/marketer/segments/lead-scoring/
- **Settings → AI agent** (/settings/agent)
- **Settings → AI configuration** (/settings/ai)
- **Settings → Apps** (/settings/apps)
